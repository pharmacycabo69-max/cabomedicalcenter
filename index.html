Good to Know

Answers before you ask.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?+

Urgent Care accepts walk-ins 24/7. All other departments recommend booking ahead, though same-day slots are often available.

Do you accept my international health insurance?+

We don't bill international insurers directly. You pay at time of visit and receive an itemized invoice formatted for reimbursement claims.

Is there a female physician available?+

Yes — gynecology is led by a female physician, and you're welcome to request one for other departments where available.

Can you send results to my doctor at home?+

Yes — with your consent, we email results and clinical summaries directly to your primary physician or insurer.

How fast can the mobile team reach my hotel or villa?+

Typical response time is 20–45 minutes across Cabo San Lucas, San José del Cabo, and The Corridor, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Is mobile IV therapy safe, and who administers it?+

All infusions are given by licensed nurses under physician-approved protocols, and our mobile division is COFEPRIS-licensed (License No. COFEPRIS-05-022-B) and meets Mexican regulatory standards.
